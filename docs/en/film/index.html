How it was made

The film is recorded frame by frame from the pages of the site itself, with the figures of issue 2, published on 19 October 2026, so every figure in it is a published figure that can be quoted from its page.

The six photographs in its opening are NASA photographs from the International Space Station, in the public domain, held still as they are with no generated motion.

The voice in both languages and the music are generated with ElevenLabs, as the closing card says, while the site itself uses no generated material.

An independent open project whose figures describe public configuration only and are not an audit of any organisation.

A new issue comes out every Monday morning.
