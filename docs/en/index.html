Arabian Gulf, measured

An open weekly measurement of how well the public domains of the six Arabian Gulf states are protected. This is issue 1, published on 16 October 2026.

Check your domain

The issue in one sentence

Of 10,084 live domains in the Arabian Gulf, 82.7% can be spoofed in email and 3.3% pass DNSSEC validation.
