Arabian Gulf, measured

An open weekly measurement of how well the public domains of the six Arabian Gulf states are protected. This is issue 3, published on 26 October 2026.

Check your domain

The issue in one sentence

Of 13,810 live domains in the Arabian Gulf, 80.7% can be spoofed in email and 2.7% pass DNSSEC validation.

What we measure

  • 13 standards in four groups, from the foundations to the frontier Email DNS Websites Vulnerability disclosure
  • 36 domain suffixes across Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman, from gov.kw to net.om

We read what any visitor sees from outside, touch no server and need no permission.

We name no organisation and rank no state.

  1. First we gather the names.

    From Certificate Transparency logs and the Tranco list, 19,069 names in this issue.

  2. Then we read what anyone sees.

    208,164 DNS queries through two public resolvers, and 11,830 websites read from servers outside the Arabian Gulf.

  3. Then we look again.

    The two resolvers agreed on 99.9% of the DMARC answers, and every inconclusive result is measured again before the run ends.

  4. On Monday, the issue.

    Totals only, with no organisation named, and the open data as CSV and JSON with an archive of every issue.
