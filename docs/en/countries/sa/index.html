Saudi Arabia

7,668 live domains measured in this issue: 397 government, 1,263 universities and schools, 203 healthcare, 998 companies, 4,321 non-profit and 486 network.

The issue in one sentence

Of 7,668 live domains in Saudi Arabia, 86.1% can be spoofed in email and 4.9% pass DNSSEC validation.
